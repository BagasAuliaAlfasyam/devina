# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS for a fast, SEO-friendly personal portfolio.

## Users

Primary users are recruiters, potential collaborators, mentors, and people reviewing Devina's transition into technology on desktop or mobile. They need to quickly understand her current level, transferable experience, and what she is learning.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Devina Hindriastika's learning journey, basic IT experience, work history, and contribution as Technical Support at Catalyst Forge. Success means a visitor can understand her current stage and see credible potential without overstated claims.

## Positioning

The portfolio leads with a clear point of view and project outcomes rather than a generic resume dump.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Initial surface: a single-page portfolio with hero, learning/project entries, experience timeline, capabilities, and a connection CTA.
- Content should remain honest about Devina's beginner level and be expanded with real project details over time.
- Must work responsively and remain accessible by keyboard.

## Evidence on Hand

No project case studies, profile image, testimonials, or brand assets were provided yet. The initial UI uses clearly marked starter content and abstract visual treatments rather than fabricated client claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the work understandable at a glance.
- Show taste through the interface without hiding the content.
- Keep the path to contact obvious.
- Prefer specific, honest evidence over inflated claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and responsive layouts that remain usable without hover.
